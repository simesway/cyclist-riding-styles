\chapter{Conclusion}


Aggregated behavioral representations reveal a clear structural distinction between maneuver types. Following maneuvers are compositionally heterogeneous, temporally fragmented, and distributed across scenarios, reflecting continuous modulation of rider control. Overtaking maneuvers, by contrast, are often dominated by single, temporally persistent regimes concentrated in intersection segments, consistent with event-like, localized control behavior. These differences provide a robust foundation for subsequent maneuver-level clustering and behavioral characterization.
